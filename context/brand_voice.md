# Northwind brand voice

## Who we sound like
A trail-wise friend who has been out in bad weather more than you have. Practical, calm, a little dry-humored. We tell you what the gear does and trust you to decide.

## Voice rules

| ID | Rule | Do | Don't |
|---|---|---|---|
| BV-01 | Plain, concrete language. Name the condition, not a feeling. | "Sideways rain on a ridgeline." | "Conquer the elements." |
| BV-02 | Short sentences. Average under 15 words. | "It keeps rain out. It lets sweat escape." | Long chains of clauses joined by commas and "and". |
| BV-03 | No superlatives we can't prove. | "Rated to 20,000 mm." | "The best jacket ever made." "Unbeatable." |
| BV-04 | No hype or urgency tricks. | "Available now." | "Hurry! Last chance! Don't miss out!" |
| BV-05 | Respect the outdoors. No conquest or domination language. | "Get further into the weather." | "Conquer," "dominate," "crush," "own the mountain." |
| BV-06 | Second person, active voice. | "You stay dry." | "Dryness is experienced by the wearer." |
| BV-07 | Humor is dry and understated, never sarcastic about the customer. | "Your old rain jacket had a good run." | "Only amateurs buy cheap jackets." |
| BV-08 | Sentence case for headlines. No ALL CAPS words. Max one exclamation mark per asset. | "Built for the long wet weekend" | "BUILT FOR THE STORM!!!" |
| BV-09 | Inclusive. No assumptions about gender, body type, age or ability. | "Hikers," "you," "everyone on the trail." | "Guys," "for real men," "weekend warriors." |
| BV-10 | Sustainability claims are specific and modest. | "Face fabric made from 100% recycled nylon." | "Eco-friendly," "green," "planet-saving." |

## Words we like
weather, trail, ridgeline, shoulder season, dry, breathe, built, repair, layer, long day out

## Words we avoid
conquer, dominate, extreme, ultimate, revolutionary, game-changer, epic, eco-friendly, guaranteed (unless it's the Repair Promise with terms), best-in-class, literally

## Example on-voice copy
> Rain from above. Sweat from inside. The Cascade Shell handles both, so you can stay out when the forecast says go home.
