# Campaign brief: Cascade Shell fall launch

- **Brand:** Northwind (fictional outdoor-gear company)
- **Product:** Northwind Cascade Shell, a 3-layer waterproof hiking jacket
- **Launch window:** October 2026
- **Audience:** Weekend and shoulder-season hikers, 28-50, who hike in wet climates (Pacific Northwest, New England, UK). They already own a cheap rain jacket and are tired of getting wet from the inside (sweat) or the outside (leaks).
- **Objective:** Drive consideration and first purchases in the first 6 weeks.
- **Key message:** Stay dry from both sides: storm-proof outside, breathable inside, built to be repaired, not replaced.
- **Proof points:** See `approved_claims.md`. Use only claims listed there.
- **Channels:** Google Search (Responsive Search Ad), Google Display (Responsive Display Ad), launch email.
- **Call to action:** "Shop the Cascade Shell"
- **Landing page:** northwind.example/cascade (placeholder domain)
- **Mandatories:** Price ($249) in email. Mention the Northwind Repair Promise in at least one asset.
