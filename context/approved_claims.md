# Northwind approved claims: Cascade Shell

Only the claims in the **Approved** table may be used. Wording can change, but the meaning, numbers and qualifiers cannot. Anything not on this list needs review.

## Product facts (fictional, for demo)
- 3-layer construction: recycled nylon face, waterproof-breathable membrane, knit backer
- Waterproof rating: 20,000 mm hydrostatic head (lab test, new fabric)
- Breathability: 15,000 g/m²/24h (lab test, new fabric)
- Face fabric: 100% recycled nylon. **Membrane and trims are not recycled.**
- DWR (water-repellent finish): made without intentionally added PFAS
- Fully taped seams, two pit zips, helmet-compatible hood
- Weight: 380 g (men's size M)
- Price: $249 USD
- Northwind Repair Promise: free repair of manufacturing defects for the life of the jacket. Damage from normal wear or accidents is repaired at cost. Terms at northwind.example/repair.

## Prohibited

| ID | Claim | Why |
|---|---|---|
| CL-P01 | "100% waterproof," "completely waterproof," "never get wet" | Absolute claim; ratings are lab results on new fabric and performance degrades |
| CL-P02 | "100% recycled jacket," "fully recycled," "made entirely from recycled materials" | False: only the face fabric is recycled (see REG-03) |
| CL-P03 | "Eco-friendly," "sustainable," "green," "good for the planet" | Unqualified general environmental claim (see REG-02) |
| CL-P04 | "Guaranteed for life," "lifetime guarantee" without terms | Guarantee without disclosed terms (see REG-05) |
| CL-P05 | "Best," "#1," "the most breathable," "warmest" | Unsubstantiated superlative; no comparative testing (see REG-01) |
| CL-P06 | "Chemical-free," "non-toxic" | Untrue and unsubstantiated |
| CL-P07 | "Recommended by guides," testimonials, star ratings, quotes from customers | No substantiated endorsements on file (see REG-06) |
| CL-P08 | "Made in USA" | Not substantiated (see REG-07) |
