# Regulations: plain-language rules for marketing copy

**Not legal advice.** These are simplified summaries for a demo guardrail. A real deployment would have legal review of every rule.

| ID | Rule | What it means for Northwind copy | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| REG-01 | Claims must be truthful and substantiated | Every factual claim needs evidence on file before it runs. No superlatives without comparative testing. | FTC Act Section 5; FTC Policy Statement on Advertising Substantiation |
| REG-02 | No unqualified general environmental claims | "Eco-friendly," "green," "sustainable" on their own are deceptive. Name the specific attribute. | FTC Green Guides, 16 CFR 260.4 |
| REG-03 | Recycled-content claims must be accurate about which part | If only part of a product is recycled, say which part. | FTC Green Guides, 16 CFR 260.13 |
| REG-04 | "Free of" claims must be accurate | Don't claim a product is free of a substance if it contains it, or "chemical-free" at all. | FTC Green Guides, 16 CFR 260.9 |
| REG-05 | Guarantees must disclose their terms | "Lifetime guarantee" or "promise" needs the material terms or a clear pointer to them. | FTC Guides Against Deceptive Advertising of Guarantees, 16 CFR 239 |
| REG-06 | Endorsements and reviews must be real and disclosed | No invented testimonials, reviews or expert endorsements. Material connections (paid, gifted) must be disclosed. | FTC Endorsement Guides, 16 CFR 255; FTC rule on consumer reviews and testimonials, 16 CFR 465 |
| REG-07 | "Made in USA" must be all or virtually all | Don't use it unless substantiated. | FTC Made in USA Labeling Rule, 16 CFR 323 |
| REG-08 | Commercial email requirements | No misleading subject lines or sender info. Include a valid physical postal address and a working unsubscribe link; honor opt-outs within 10 business days. | CAN-SPAM Act, 15 U.S.C. 7704; 16 CFR 316 |
| REG-09 | Disclose AI-generated realistic imagery | Photorealistic AI-generated images of people, places or events must be labeled as AI-generated. Northwind applies this everywhere as house policy. | EU AI Act Article 50 (applies from Aug 2, 2026) |
